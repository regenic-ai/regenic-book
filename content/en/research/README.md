# Public research policy

Regenic uses evidence to test mechanisms and boundaries, not to decorate a
prewritten conclusion.

- Pair positive and negative cases under comparable constraints where possible.
- Label each claim as independently verified, organization-reported, or a
  hypothesis.
- Preserve failures and contradictory evidence.
- Separate observed facts from interpretation.
- Do not publish internal operating records or third-party confidential data.

Browse the public index at `/en/research` and `/zh/research`.
